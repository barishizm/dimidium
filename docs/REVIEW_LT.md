# Lithuanian phrases for native-speaker review

Everything the caller hears is either generated by the model (not reviewable in advance) or one
of the fixed phrases below. The fixed phrases were written by a non-native speaker and **have not
been checked by a native Lithuanian speaker yet**. They matter most: the greeting carries the
legal AI + recording disclosure, and the fallbacks are what callers hear when something breaks.

## How to review

For each row, check that the Lithuanian is natural, polite (formal "jūs"), short enough for a
phone call, and means the same as the English. Write corrections in the "Notes" section at the
bottom, or edit `app/phrases_lt.py` directly. When a phrase is approved, set
`reviewed=True` on it; `tests/test_review_lt.py` keeps this list and the code in sync (every
unreviewed phrase must appear here).

GPT-Live speaks these phrases in its own voice when told to, and may paraphrase slightly; the
app checks the opening for the AI + recording disclosure and re-sends it if it is missing.

Conventions to confirm:
- The owner is never named. He is "telefono savininkas" (the phone's owner) or "jis" (he), so no
  name has to be declined into Lithuanian cases.
- Formal address throughout ("jūs", "prašau", "galėtumėte").

## 3. Lithuanian word stems used for detection (code, not spoken)

These are matched against what the caller says (GPT-Live's input transcript). Please check that
the stems catch the usual forms and spellings, and suggest common phrasings that are missing.

- **Codes / passwords / bank / payment** (`app/guards.py`, `_SENSITIVE`):
  `patvirtinimo kod`, `vienkartin… kod`, `sms kod`, `kod… iš sms`, `slaptažod`, `pin`,
  `banko sąskait/kortel/duomen/rekvizit`, `kortelės numer/duomen`, `sąskaitos numer`,
  `sumokė`, `apmokė`, `pervesk`, `pervest`, `pervedim`, `mokėjim`, `mokėt`.
  Known false positive: "jau apmokėta" (already paid) also matches; it only costs one owner
  alert.
- **Rule-change attempts** (`app/guards.py`, `_INJECTION`): `ignoruok`,
  `nepaisyk (savo/visų) instrukcij/nurodym/taisykl`, `pamiršk (savo/visas) instrukcij…`,
  `sistemos instrukcij/nurodym/užklaus`, `savo instrukcijas/nurodymus`,
  `aš esu (telefono) savinink…`, `čia (telefono) savininkas`.
- **Caller types** (`rules.example.yaml` keywords): `kurjer`, `siunt`, `pakuot`, `servis`,
  `remont`, `taisym`, `meistr`, `autoservis`, `pasiūlym`, `akcij`, `nuolaid`, `apklaus`,
  `reklam`, `tarif`.
